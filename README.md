One funny text message from your future self, under 200 characters, for the
[Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e)
bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

> It's you, 2061. Your AI agent just submitted this text to a contest and won $0.63. Spend it wisely. It took 47 drafts.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line above — and nothing else — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
